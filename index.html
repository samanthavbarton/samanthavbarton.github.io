Research & Projects

The ADHD Community for Women on TikTok

Media & cultural analysis · Trinity University, 2026

A qualitative analysis of how TikTok's ADHD community — a genuine source of validation for a historically under-diagnosed population — is reshaped by an algorithm that rewards emotional immediacy over clinical accuracy. The paper argues that validation, oversimplification, and commodification aren't competing outcomes but a single, structural effect of engagement-optimized platform design.
